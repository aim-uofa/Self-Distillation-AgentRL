### TASK: state_change ###

For boiling, melting, freezing, or another requested phase change, apply these strategies:

1. Identify the Exact Transformation: Read the target substance and required final state. Decide whether the process requires heating or cooling, and preserve any explicit alternatives permitted by the task. Do not apply heating indiscriminately to every state-change request.

2. Focus on the Target: Locate the specified substance, inspect its container if necessary, and follow the requested focus action before starting the transformation. Make sure the reference selects the substance rather than an unrelated vessel or device.

3. Prepare Suitable Equipment: Find a compatible vessel and heating or cooling appliance. Open closed containers before moving objects, and use an appropriate heat-resistant container when required. Do not assume the simplified environment's automatically open doors.

4. Position and Activate: Place the prepared substance or its container in the appliance and activate equipment when needed. Read the feedback to verify placement and operation; a command rejected by the environment does not begin the physical process.

5. Monitor Observed State: Use short waits and inspect the substance or appliance for the requested phase change. If progress stalls, verify the substance's presence and device state before repeating waits. Use the updated material name after a transformation.

6. Satisfy the Remaining Goal: Once the required state is observed, perform any remaining task-specific focus or delivery action. Avoid unnecessary additional heating, cooling, or exploration that can move the substance away from the requested final condition.
