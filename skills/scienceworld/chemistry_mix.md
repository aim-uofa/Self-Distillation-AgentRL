### TASK: chemistry_mix ###

For mixing substances and paint colors, apply these strategies:

1. Determine the Requested Product: Read the target substance or paint color and locate any recipe or instructions supplied by the environment. Use `read` on the relevant document rather than guessing a procedure from unrelated previous mixtures.

2. Gather the Correct Ingredients: Identify each required ingredient by its observed name and find a suitable mixing container. Collect the necessary items before mixing, and distinguish the chemical contents from the vessels that currently hold them.

3. Put Ingredients in One Vessel: Move solids or pour liquids into the intended container using the appropriate command. If water is needed from a sink, position the vessel, activate the source, then deactivate and retrieve it as appropriate. Verify the resulting contents.

4. Avoid Accidental Contamination: Check that the vessel contains the intended combination before issuing `mix CONTAINER`. Do not repeatedly mix incomplete ingredients or combine unrelated products merely because their containers are nearby.

5. Respect Multi-Stage Recipes: When an intermediate mixture is required, make and identify that intermediate before adding the next ingredient. For paint tasks, track the requested color and observed products, rather than relying on a fixed sequence of container names.

6. Verify and Report the Product: Inspect the resulting contents and perform any requested final focus or placement action on the new substance. Successful mixing feedback alone is insufficient if the task still requires a specific final selection or destination.
